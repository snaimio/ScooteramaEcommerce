Scooterama — Where Style Meets Motion.

Who is Scooterama?

Scooterama rides at the front of California's urban movement, mixing bold design with a laid-back West Coast spirit. Born in the streets where surf culture meets tech culture, the company set out with a simple idea: scooters should feel as cool as they look. Their lineup blends sleek engineering, vibrant colorways, and whisper-quiet motors that glide through the city like a vibe on wheels. Whether you're commuting, exploring, or just cruising for the joy of it, Scooterama turns everyday travel into a small adventure.

What really sets Scooterama apart is the personality baked into every ride. This is a brand that celebrates creativity, independence, and a little playful rebellion—exactly what California does best. Riders don't just get from point A to point B; they join a community that values freedom, style, and clean energy. With events, pop-up meets, and collaborations with local artists, Scooterama has become the scooter equivalent of a cool neighborhood café: effortlessly hip, unmistakably local, and always in motion.

Our Products

Urban Mobility App

A mobile application that helps users find the best scooter routes through city traffic. Features real-time traffic updates, charging station locations, and route optimization for the fastest and safest paths.

Built with React Native and integrated with Google Maps API. The app reduces average commute time by 23% according to user feedback from our beta test in San Francisco.

Scooter Safety Dashboard

An analytics dashboard for city planners to monitor scooter usage patterns and identify high-risk areas. Includes heat maps, accident reporting, and usage statistics.

Developed with D3.js and Node.js, this tool has been adopted by three major California cities to improve urban planning and reduce scooter-related incidents by 18%.

Eco-Charge Network

A network of solar-powered charging stations for electric scooters. Each station generates its own power and provides free charging to Scooterama users.

Currently deployed in 15 locations across Los Angeles and San Diego. The project has offset over 5,000 kg of CO2 emissions in its first year of operation.
